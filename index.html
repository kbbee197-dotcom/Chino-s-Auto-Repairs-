Our Story

Built from nothing. Driven by love for the work.

Chino grew up moving between Wilmington, Dover, and New Castle, Delaware — a home-grown Delawarean who learned early that nothing gets handed to you. What he built at Chino's Auto Repair & Mechanic Shop wasn't inherited or funded; it was earned one engine, one estimate, and one satisfied customer at a time.

Our Mission

To deliver honest, high-quality auto care that working families and everyday people can actually afford — and to make a difference in our community, one car at a time.
